About this Episode

In this episode of it will probably be okay… in the wake of the “Big Game” on Sunday … today’s topic is SPORTS!

Today, each of us will take our own unique look at these children’s games which occupy a massive place in our national and truly worldwide conversation. Maybe too large a place. So grab your glove, put on your helmet and let us diagram today’s play … a discussion, or actually three discussions, of sports.
