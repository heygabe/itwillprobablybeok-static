About this Episode

In this Episode of It Will Probably Be OK, Nkenge talks through the ideas of defunding and abolishing the police. Additionally, Nick wistfully remembers making those cool S’s, and Gabe derails the whole conversation with so he can talk about the movie “Elf.”

Mini Topics:

  • Nick enjoys the television program “Dave” on the FX Network.
  • Gabe wants someone to paint his gold house.
